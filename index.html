Welcome to Paradise Jiu Jitsu and Muay Thai

We are a small martial arts academy focused on Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Muay Thai, fitness, and self-defense. Our classes are made for beginners as well as experienced students.

Our goal is to create a friendly place where people can learn useful skills, get in better shape, and improve one class at a time.

Our Mission

Our mission is to teach practical martial arts in a safe and respectful environment. We want every student to feel welcome, whether they are training for fun, fitness, self-defense, or competition.
